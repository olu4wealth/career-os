# Career OS — User Guide

Everything you need to use Career OS, step by step. No technical knowledge required.

**What it is:** your personal command centre for becoming excellent as a
Future, Growth & Monitoring Officer. It helps you track business performance,
understand *why* it changes, spot opportunities, make recommendations — and get
visibly better at the job week after week.

**Start here:** [https://olu4wealth.github.io/career-os/](https://olu4wealth.github.io/career-os/)
(open it now — the rest of this guide makes more sense with it in front of you).

## 1. Three ways to use Career OS

Pick whichever fits. The screens are identical — only where your data is kept differs.

### A. The website (easiest, works anywhere)

Open **https://olu4wealth.github.io/career-os/** on any laptop, tablet, or phone.
Nothing to install. Your entries are saved **inside that browser** — so your
laptop and your phone each keep their own copy. They do not sync.

### B. The desktop app (best for daily work on your PC)

Double-click the **Career OS** shortcut on your desktop. Your browser opens
with the app, and your data is saved in a real database file next to the app.
Close the black window that appears if you like — actually, don't: that window
*is* the app running. Just minimise it. To stop the app, close that window.

> If Windows SmartScreen warns you the first time ("Unknown publisher"), click
> **More info → Run anyway**. The app was built on your own machine; it sends
> nothing anywhere — you can verify this yourself because every line of code
> is public on GitHub.

### C. The Python version (for technical users)

```
python server.py
```

then open http://127.0.0.1:8130. Same app, same database. This is also how
developers run it from the source code.

**Which should you use?** Use the **desktop app** for your real daily work
(safest storage). Use the **website** when you're away from your PC or showing
someone the concept.

## 2. Your first 10 minutes

The app opens with sample insurance data so you can see how everything works.
These numbers are **examples, not your company's figures** — you will replace
them with your own in step 4.

1. **Look at the Command Centre.** Within 30 seconds you should see: today's
   main goal ("boss fight"), four business numbers, a red/amber alert list,
   opportunities, latest intelligence, and your XP level. This page is your
   morning briefing — come here first every day.
2. **Click through the left sidebar.** There are 13 sections (each is explained
   in [section 5](#5-guide-to-each-section)). Don't memorise them; just notice
   what's there.
3. **Try dark mode.** Click **◑ Dark** at the bottom of the sidebar. Click
   again to go back. Your choice is remembered.
4. **Replace one sample number with a real one.** Go to **KPI Cockpit**, click
   the first row, change Actual/Target to figures from your latest report, and
   Save. Congratulations — it is now *your* system, not a demo.
5. **Set tomorrow's boss fight.** Go to **Settings**, type one outcome for
   tomorrow (see examples below), Save.

**What makes a good boss fight?** One high-value *outcome*, not a task list:

- Weak: "Work on Motor report."
- Strong: "Determine why September Motor premium is below target and produce three actionable recommendations."

## 3. The daily workflow

This is the routine the whole app is designed around. Total overhead: minutes.

### Morning (5 min): open the Command Centre

- Read your **boss fight** and top 3 priorities.
- Scan **Business pulse**: which numbers moved? Red and amber items need you.
- Read **Attention required**: overdue items, deteriorating KPIs, risks.
- Glance at **Market intelligence**: anything that affects today's work?

### Mid-morning: Daily OS → Focus mode

- Open **Daily OS**. Your outcomes for the day are listed with XP rewards.
- When it's time for deep work, click **◎ Focus mode**. Only your current
  task and a timer remain on screen. Click **Start**.
- Got distracted? Click **+ Distraction** (honesty counts — it just counts).
- Finished the block? Click **Complete (+XP)**. You earn 10 XP for a
  protected deep-work block.

### During analysis: from number → insight → recommendation

This is the most important workflow in the app — the **Track → Analyse →
Identify → Explain → Recommend** chain from your job description:

1. In **KPI Cockpit**, spot a red metric (say Motor premium −10% vs target).
2. Click the row. Fill in **Driver** (why it happened), **Business
   implication** (why it matters), and **Recommended action**.
3. Still need to think it through? Click **＋ Insight builder** and answer the
   8 guided questions (what changed → how significant → why → root cause →
   so what → what should management do → who → by when). Clicking
   **Generate management insight** saves it to your permanent log and earns 25 XP.
4. Open the **Insight Log** anytime to re-read, search, or update the outcome
   (Pending → Adopted/Rejected). Over months this becomes your personal
   database of business knowledge — the asset that makes you promotable.

### When you spot growth: Opportunity Radar

Heard a broker ask for fleet pricing twice? Don't let it evaporate:

1. Click **+ Add opportunity**. Write what you saw under **Evidence**
   (evidence is compulsory — hunches without evidence stay ideas).
2. Rate **potential value**, **feasibility**, and **strategic fit**.
3. Set the **next validation step** (e.g. "Price 2 pilot bundles").
4. Move it along the stages: Idea → Research → Validate → Business Case →
   Pilot → Scale. The matrix at the top shows you at a glance which ideas are
   both valuable *and* doable — that's where your energy goes.

### When something happens outside: Market Intelligence

Read a NAICOM circular or a competitor launch? Capture it in under a minute:
headline, what changed, **why it matters to us**, and the recommended action.
The app forces the one discipline that matters: never file information without
writing down its implication. High-priority items surface on your Command Centre.

### Evening (3 min): close the loop

- In **Daily OS**, click **Done** on finished outcomes (XP is awarded automatically).
- Write tomorrow's boss fight in **Settings**.
- Leave with an empty head: everything is tracked, nothing is floating.

## 4. The weekly review (Fridays)

Open **Weekly Review** and click **+ New review**. Answer honestly:

- What happened in the business? What moved materially?
- What deteriorated? What opportunity emerged?
- Which initiatives are late or at risk, and why?
- What did *I* accomplish, learn, and improve?

Then score yourself 1–5 on the six Friday-check questions (spotted a material
change? explained why? found an opportunity or risk? moved an initiative?
made a recommendation? protected deep work?). The page shows your week's XP,
closed tasks, insights, and open alerts alongside — evidence, not vibes.

## 5. Guide to each section

| # | Section | What it's for | The one habit that makes it work |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Command Centre | Your 30-second morning briefing | Open it first, every day, before email |
| 2 | Daily OS | Outcomes, focus timer, rhythm | One boss fight; mark Done honestly |
| 3 | KPI Cockpit | Actual vs target, variance, drivers | Every red KPI gets an owner + next action |
| 4 | Insight Log | Permanent knowledge base | Log the outcome (Adopted/Rejected), not just the idea |
| 5 | Strategic Initiatives | Track projects to deadline | Update status + next action weekly; escalate early |
| 6 | Opportunity Radar | Growth pipeline | No evidence, no opportunity — validate in stages |
| 7 | Market Intelligence | External signals with implications | Every signal needs a "why it matters" |
| 8 | Competitors | Move → our implication → response | Mystery-shop one competitor flow per month |
| 9 | Weekly Review | Business + self review | Friday, 30 minutes, scores honest |
| 10 | Skill Tree | 11 career skills with levels | Advance one level per quarter per active skill |
| 11 | XP & Progress | Levels, streaks, history | Claim XP the same day you earn it |
| 12 | Templates | Performance analysis, exec slide, intel, opportunity, initiative | Use one instead of starting from blank |
| 13 | Settings | Boss fight, priorities, data tools | Set tomorrow's fight before leaving |

**Field tips worth knowing:**

- **KPI Cockpit** — *Variance* and *Variance %* are calculated for you
  (Actual − Target). *YoY* needs last year's figure in *Prior year*.
  Leave *Status* blank and the app grades it: within 3% green, 3–8% amber,
  beyond that red.
- **Strategic Initiatives** — overdue items appear automatically on the
  Command Centre. Set *Escalation* to Yes and it gets flagged loudly.
- **XP & Progress** — the *Reward guide* lists every earning action with a
  **Claim** button. *Quality ×* multiplies XP for exceptional work; *streak
  bonus* adds extra for unbroken daily streaks.
- **Skill Tree** — *Level* is where you are (1–5), *Target* is where you're
  going. The bar shows progress. *Evidence of mastery* is the proof you'd
  show a manager.

## 6. The XP and levels system

XP rewards **outputs, not hours**. One level every 200 XP. Streaks count
consecutive days with any XP earned.

| What you did | XP |
|---|---|
| Completed routine report | 10 |
| Protected a distraction-free deep-work block | 10 |
| Completed difficult analysis | 20 |
| Found a material business insight | 25 |
| Identified a viable growth opportunity | 30 |
| Delivered a management-ready recommendation | 30 |
| Automated a repetitive workflow | 40 |
| Manager acted on your recommendation | 50 |

Rules of the game: claim XP the day you earn it, never award yourself XP for
attending meetings or "being busy", and let a broken streak break — the
comeback streak feels better.

## 7. Importing, exporting, backup

- **Moving from Excel:** open your workbook, save a sheet as CSV, then in the
  matching section click **Import CSV**. Columns must match the app's columns —
  click **Export CSV** first to get a correctly-headed template.
- **Per-section export:** every table has **Export CSV**.
- **Full backup (do this weekly):** Settings → **Download full backup**.
  It saves one JSON file with everything. The Command Centre shows a reminder
  banner if you haven't backed up in 7+ days. There is no undo and no cloud
  copy — this file *is* your safety net. Keep it somewhere safe.
- **Starting over:** Settings → **Reset demo data** (asks for confirmation).
  On the desktop app this restores fresh sample data; online it clears your
  browser copy and reloads the samples.

## 8. Settings, dark mode, search

- **Settings** holds your boss fight, top 3 priorities, and all data tools.
- **Dark mode** (◑ Dark, bottom of sidebar) follows you — it syncs with nothing,
  it's per browser, and the charts recolour to match.
- **Search everything** with **Ctrl K** (or Cmd K on Mac): KPIs, insights,
  initiatives, opportunities, intel, tasks. Click a result to open it.
- Keyboard: **Esc** closes any dialog. Table rows open with **Enter**.

## 9. Troubleshooting

| Problem | Fix |
|---|---|
| Double-clicked the shortcut and nothing happens | Wait 10 seconds (first start is slower), then check http://127.0.0.1:8130 manually. If a "port in use" error appears, an old copy is still running — close its black window and try again. |
| Windows warns about the exe | Click More info → Run anyway (it's your own code; source is public). |
| Blank white page online | Hard-refresh (Ctrl F5). If it persists, the deployed version may be rebuilding — wait a minute. |
| My online entries disappeared | Browser data was cleared (cache clean, new browser, incognito). Restore from your backup file's data by re-entering — and back up weekly from now on. |
| Numbers look wrong in a chart | Charts compare against *Target*. A missing target (0/blank) distorts scales — fill in targets for every KPI. |
| I messed up the sample data beyond recognition | Settings → Reset demo data. |
| CSV import says 0 rows imported | The header row must match the app's column names exactly — export first and use that file as your template. |

## 10. Questions people ask

**Is my company data private?**
Yes — by architecture, not by promise. The desktop app never leaves your PC
(no account, no network calls except to itself). The website stores data only
in your own browser. There is nowhere for data to leak *to*.

**Why are there two versions (website vs desktop)?**
The website host (GitHub Pages) can only show pages, not run a database — so
online, your browser is the database. Same screens, same features; the desktop
app just keeps the data in a real file instead. See the table in section 1.

**Can I share it with a colleague?**
Yes — send them the website link or this guide. Note the MIT license: they can
use and adapt it freely. Their data stays theirs.

**Can it connect to our company database / Power BI / Excel live?**
Not yet — that's on the roadmap. Today the bridge is CSV import/export. The
API is deliberately shaped so those integrations slot in later.

**Does it work on my phone?**
The website opens on phones and tablets; Daily OS, alerts, tasks, and XP are
the most useful parts there. Desktop is the primary experience.

**I broke something — is there support?**
It's a personal open-source project: check this guide, then Troubleshooting,
then open an issue on the GitHub repo with what you clicked and what happened.

## 11. Mini-glossary

- **Variance** — Actual minus Target. Negative means below target.
- **Variance %** — that gap as a percentage of target. −10% means ten percent short.
- **YoY (year-on-year)** — this period vs the same period last year. Filters out seasonality.
- **Retention** — share of existing customers who renewed. Falling retention + steady new business = running to stand still.
- **Claims ratio / loss ratio** — claims paid as a share of premium. Lower is (usually) better.
- **Attainment** — actual as a percentage of target. 100 = exactly on target.
- **Boss fight** — gamer slang, reclaimed: the one high-value outcome that makes the day a win.
- **XP** — experience points. Here: proof of outputs, not hours.
